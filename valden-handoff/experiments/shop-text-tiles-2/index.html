Text tiles, 5 more ideas

Same dark card and grid as V3. Now the two text tiles get playful: they become little objects. Separate experiment, main page untouched.

C1 · Fill lineA coffee-coloured wave fills the bottom of the tile and slowly drifts. The word sits above the line like a cup being filled.

C2 · Till receiptEach tile is a mini café receipt: items with dotted leaders and a total. The type stays small and tidy, so it is the quietest.

C3 · Spinning sealA round seal with text running around the edge that turns very slowly, and a big number in the middle.

C4 · Sliced wordOne big word cut in half, with the halves pushed apart sideways, like a glitch or a split-flap sign.

C5 · Letter blocksA word spelled out as four chunky letter blocks, each tilted, like alphabet toys.
