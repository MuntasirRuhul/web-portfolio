Shop section, 5 ideas

Same four products, no plain card grid. A and C are the closest to how people buy coffee in a shop. B, D and E are more playful. These are working mockups: tap or hover, and every plus button adds a demo item.

A · The shelfTins stand on one wooden-style ledge against a blush wall, with paper price labels pinned under each. It looks like a real shop shelf, and each tin lifts on hover.

B · Sliding panelsFour colour panels side by side. Tap one and it opens wide to show the tin, notes and price, while the others fold to a vertical name.

C · Menu boardA café menu: names, dotted leaders, prices. Hover or tap a row and the tin on the right changes colour and swaps.

D · Pick by moodIt asks how you take your morning. Pick an answer and one tin is recommended, big, with a short reason. Choosing becomes the interaction.

E · Featured gift boxThe gift box leads as one large tile, since it's the best value. The three single tins sit in a compact stack beside it.
