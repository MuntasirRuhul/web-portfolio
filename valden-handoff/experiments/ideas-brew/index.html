Brew Bar, 5 ideas

Same job (pick a method and cups, get the recipe), five different feels. All are interactive. Tips are neutral now, so they no longer name individual blends.

A · The order slipThe recipe prints as a café order slip with a torn edge: dotted leaders, a total line, a short tip.

B · One sentenceNo tiles. The recipe is a sentence you edit inline: pick the method, change the cups, and the numbers in the sentence change.

C · Live brew timerIt stops being a calculator and guides the brew: a countdown ring, the steps lighting up in turn, and start/pause/reset.

D · Measuring glassThe glass becomes the main thing. It fills as you add cups, with ml marks on the side, and the recipe pinned beside it on tape.

E · Compare all fourNo method to pick. All four recipes sit side by side and update together from one cup stepper, so you can compare time and grind at a glance.
