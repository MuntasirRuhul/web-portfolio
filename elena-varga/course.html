01About

Learn to look before you draw.

Most people who think they can’t draw were never taught to look. This course starts there: you train your eye on shapes, values and edges first, then draw along with Elena as she works.

You need no experience and very few materials. By week six you’ll have one finished, considered drawing of a subject you chose, and a private video from Elena on what to try next.

What you’ll learn

  • See shapes, values and edges instead of “things”
  • Measure proportions with only your pencil
  • Model light and shadow with three values
  • Use gesture to put life into a drawing fast
  • Control lost and found edges for depth
  • Plan and finish one drawing from start to end

How each topic works

  1. Step 01~10 min
    LookA short exercise that trains your eye before you make a mark.Exercise · eye training
  2. Step 02~25 min
    SketchDraw along in real time while Elena explains each decision.Video lesson · draw along
  3. Step 03~30 min
    FinishComplete a practice piece and compare it with hers.Practice piece · yours to keep

One topic ≈ 1 hour

Who it’s for

  • Complete beginners, including anyone who stopped drawing at school
  • Painters who want stronger drawing under their colour
  • Not for portraits and figures in depth. That’s Course 02.

What you’ll need

  • HB and 4B pencilsany brand
  • Kneaded eraserabout $3
  • A4 cartridge paper120 gsm
  • A desk lamplight from one side

06Before you enrol

Before you begin.

I’ve never drawn before. Is this for me?

Yes. The course assumes nothing. If you can hold a pencil and write your name, you can follow every lesson.

How long do I have to finish?

As long as you like. Most students take six weeks at about an hour a week, and access never runs out.

How does the critique work?

Upload a photo of your final drawing in topic 6. Within ten days Elena sends you a private video about it, usually 8 to 12 minutes long.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days of buying, as long as you’ve watched fewer than five lessons.
