01Principles

Five rules the eye can feel.

Every decision on every page should be traceable to one of these. If it isn’t, it’s probably noise.

  1. 01The studio is the gallery.

    Sections are things in Elena’s studio: posters, a wall, letters, labels. Never “a features grid”.

    Courses hung like posters
  2. 02Drawn, then painted.

    Structure is grey line on paper. Colour arrives late, in a few deliberate blocks.

    Look · Sketch · Paint
  3. 03Look longer.

    Generous space, slow motion, one idea per section. Nothing hurries the eye.

    140px between chapters
  4. 04The hand is present.

    A signature, an irregular paint pot, a card tilted half a degree. Never a wobble filter.

    Elena V.
  5. 05Quiet labels, loud statements.

    10.5px mono captions beside 68px sentence headlines. Nothing in between shouts.

11Archetypes

Four rooms, one house.

Every page declares what kind of room it is on <body data-archetype>. The language stays; the amount of it changes with the job.

  • Gallery

    Showing. Paper, grain, grid lines, chapter labels, display headlines, one dark room, at most one row of colour blocks.

    Home, about, courses, services, notes · See the home page

  • Reading

    Studying. A white sheet with a 760px column. Beige, grain and grid only inside bands and objects.

    Course page, article · See the course page

  • Workroom

    Doing. A quiet tool: no grain, no grid, no reveals, no display headlines. Progress in the course accent.

    Lesson, quiz, assignment, dashboard · See the lesson page

  • Counter

    Committing. One white card on paper beside a room or photo. Errors in terracotta text, one primary button.

    Log in, checkout, contact · coming with the site pages
