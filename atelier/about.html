01About ElenaAbout the Atelier

I teach people to look.We teach people to look.

I’ve painted for twenty years and taught for ten. Most people who say they can’t draw were never shown how to see. That is the only thing I really teach; the pencil comes after.The Atelier began at Elena Varga’s kitchen table in Lisbon. Today four painters teach here, but the first lesson is still the same: most people who say they can’t draw were never shown how to see.

03What I believeWhat we believe

Notes pinned above the desk.

Four sentences that have survived every move, every course, and every student who told me they couldn’t draw.

  • Look for longer than feels comfortable.

    Most mistakes happen in the first three seconds of looking, not in the hand.

    Pinned 2006
  • Three values, not seven.

    Light, shadow, dark. Everything else is detail the drawing didn’t need.

    Pinned 2012
  • Draw the shadow’s shape, not the thing.

    A cup is hard. A dark crescent next to a light oval is easy.

    Pinned 2016
  • Finished beats perfect.

    Every course ends with one drawing you finish, not ten you abandon.

    Pinned 2020
