01About ElenaAbout the Atelier

I teach people to look.We teach people to look.

I’ve painted for twenty years and taught for ten. Most people who say they can’t draw were never shown how to see. That is the only thing I really teach; the pencil comes after.The Atelier began at Elena Varga’s kitchen table in Lisbon. Today four painters teach here, but the first lesson is still the same: most people who say they can’t draw were never shown how to see.

Born Budapest, 1984Lisbon since 2012Painting since 2006Teaching since 2016

02The story

The same chair, twenty years.

In my first studio there was one chair, so I drew it. I still draw it every January. It’s the most honest record I have of learning to see.Elena drew one chair in her first studio, and still draws it every January. It’s the most honest record of learning to see that the Atelier has.

  1. 2006Contour · BudapestA borrowed room

    First studio, shared with a sculptor. I drew what I thought a chair was.

  2. 2010Gesture · LisbonMoving for the light

    A three-month residency. I learnt to draw fast, before I could think.

  3. 2014Proportion · GraçaMeasuring everything

    My first solo show was 22 studies of one table. The pencil became a ruler.

  4. 2016Value · on cameraThe first filmed lesson

    A student asked how I “actually see” a shadow. I answered with three values.

  5. 2026Finished · 12,400 studentsStill at the same table

    Same chair, ten years of teaching later. Every course ends with a drawing like this one.

Graphite on cartridge paper · 21 × 29.7 cm eachStudio collection
