Overview

Most people who "can't draw" were never taught to look. This course starts there. Each topic follows Elena's three steps, Look, Sketch, Paint, so you train your eye before you touch the paper, then draw along with her in real time.

You need no experience and very few materials. By week six you will have a finished, considered drawing of a subject you chose, and a private video from Elena on what to try next.

  • See shapes, values and edges instead of "things"
  • Measure proportions with nothing but your pencil
  • Draw a convincing light and shadow with three values
  • Use gesture to get life into a drawing fast
  • Control lost and found edges for depth
  • Plan and finish one drawing from start to end

What you'll need

  • HB and 4B graphite pencilsAny brand · about $4
  • Kneaded eraserShapes into a point for highlights
  • A4 cartridge paper, 120 gsmA pad of 20 sheets is plenty
  • A desk lampOne light source, from the side
  • A craft knife or sharpenerElena sharpens with a knife; either works

Before you enrol

I've never drawn before. Is this for me?

Yes. This course assumes nothing. If you can hold a pencil and write your name, you can follow every lesson.

How long do I have to finish?

As long as you like. Most students take six weeks at about an hour a week, but access never runs out.

How does the critique work?

Upload a photo of your final drawing in topic 6. Within ten days Elena sends you a private video going through it, usually 8 to 12 minutes long.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days of buying, as long as you've watched fewer than five lessons.
