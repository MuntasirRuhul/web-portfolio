02The artist

The Atelier captures how Elena Varga draws, paints and teaches, at her own table and at her own pace, for anyone willing to look closely.

I started filming lessons in my Lisbon studio in 2016 because students kept asking the same question: "How do you actually see that?" Ten years and twelve thousand students later, these courses are my best answer, filmed at the speed I really work.

Painting changes slowly, so the Atelier does too. New courses arrive each season, along with guest sessions from painters and illustrators I admire, from ink to oil. Get the studio notes to hear first.

07Questions

Before you begin.

I've never drawn before. Is this for me?

Yes, and especially you. Foundations of Drawing assumes nothing. If you can hold a pencil and write your name, you can learn to draw.

What materials do I need?

Each course has a downloadable materials list with budget and studio options. Most beginner courses need only a pencil, an eraser and a sketchbook.

How long do I have access?

Forever. Buy once and watch at your own pace, including all future updates to that course.

Can I get feedback on my work?

Every course includes one private video critique from me, plus access to our student community for ongoing encouragement.

Book a portfolio review

A 30-minute video call. Elena looks at three of your pieces and tells you what to try next.
