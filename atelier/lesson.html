Topic 04 · Light and value · Lesson 1 of 4

Three values, not seven

Video lesson19:40Draw along

Step 02 · Sketch

Your eye sees dozens of greys. A drawing only needs three: light, shadow and dark. In this lesson you squint until the egg breaks into three shapes, then draw those shapes and nothing else.

Draw along with Elena from 06:12. Pause whenever she does, and don’t blend yet. Blending comes in topic 5.

In this lesson

Have these ready

  • An HB and a 4B pencil, sharpened
  • A white egg on white paper
  • A desk lamp lighting it from one side
  • The three-value scale, printed (Exercise files)

A note from Elena

If you can see more than three greys, squint harder. The detail you lose is detail the drawing didn’t need.

Elena V.

After this lesson

Cast shadows (15:30), then your practice piece: an egg on white paper.
