Quick tips

Fresh beans

Use beans roasted within the last 2–6 weeks and store them airtight, away from light.

Grind just before

Grinding right before brewing preserves aroma. Burr grinders give a more even grind.

Water matters

Use filtered water heated to 90–96 °C (195–205 °F) — never fully boiling.

Weigh it

A scale beats scoops. Consistency is the fastest route to a great cup.

Taste & adjust

Sour? Grind finer or brew longer. Bitter? Grind coarser or brew shorter.

Clean gear

Oils build up and turn rancid. Rinse and wash equipment regularly.
