Frequently Asked Questions
How long does it take to see results?

That depends on where you’re starting and what you're seeking. Some people feel subtle shifts after just one session, while deeper transformation typically unfolds over 6–12 weeks.


What exactly is a holistic wellness coach?

A holistic wellness coach supports your overall wellbeing — mind, body, and soul — through lifestyle guidance, emotional support, and practical tools. It’s not about “fixing” you but about reconnecting you with your natural rhythm and strength.

Do you offer online sessions?


Yes! I primarily work online to support clients from anywhere in the world. All sessions are held over Zoom or a secure video platform.

Is this like therapy or counseling?

Coaching is different from therapy. While therapy often looks to the past for healing, coaching focuses on the present and future — helping you move forward with clarity, tools, and accountability. I can always refer you to a therapist if deeper clinical support is needed.

What topics can we work on together?

Anything related to your personal wellbeing — including stress & burnout, emotional balance, nervous system regulation, boundaries, habits, self-worth, sleep, energy, nutrition, and more.
