Ceramic Arts Mastery

This specialization course immerses you in the rich world of traditional ceramic arts. Learn foundational techniques including hand-building, wheel-throwing, surface decoration, and firing processes. Through a combination of practical lessons, quizzes, and projects, you’ll develop the skills to create timeless pottery pieces using time-honored methods.

Enrolled by

15 students
What You’ll Learn?
Historical and cultural contexts of traditional ceramics
Types and properties of clay suited to various techniques
Hand-building and wheel-throwing fundamentals
Surface decoration methods and glaze application
Kiln types, firing processes, and safety measures
Complete ceramic pieces from raw clay to finished product
Develop a personal style grounded in traditional craftsmanship
