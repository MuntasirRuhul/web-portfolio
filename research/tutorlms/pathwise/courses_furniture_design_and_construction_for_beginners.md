Furniture Design and Construction for Beginners

This beginner-friendly course introduces the fundamentals of furniture design and construction. You’ll learn how to conceptualize, design, select materials, and build simple furniture pieces using basic tools and techniques. The course balances creative design principles with practical woodworking skills, helping you transform ideas into functional and beautiful furniture.

Enrolled by

19 students
What You’ll Learn?
Historical context and styles in furniture design
Sketching and planning furniture pieces with accurate measurements
Selecting and preparing appropriate materials
Mastering basic joinery and assembly techniques
Applying surface finishes for durability and aesthetics
Building confidence to create simple functional furniture
Combining creativity and craftsmanship in your projects
