Camera Skills & Photo Theory

This beginner-friendly course introduces the fundamentals of photography, focusing on mastering your camera’s basic functions and understanding core photographic theory. Learn how to capture stunning images by controlling exposure, composition, and light. Ideal for aspiring photographers ready to move beyond auto mode and create intentional, beautiful photos.

Enrolled by

12 students
What You’ll Learn?
Camera types, controls, and functions
Exposure fundamentals: ISO, aperture, shutter speed
Essential composition and framing techniques
Basics of lighting, color theory, and white balance
Practical shooting modes and beginner editing skills
