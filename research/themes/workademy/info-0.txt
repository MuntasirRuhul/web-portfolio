Overview

Workademy is a dynamic and modern WordPress LMS theme tailored for corporate training, team learning, and individual instructors using Tutor LMS.

From onboarding new hires to launching advanced certification programs, Workademy provides the professional structure and sleek design needed to engage modern learners. With pre-built pages like Courses, Instructors, Blog, Checkout, etc, your eLearning platform is ready to launch in no time. The theme’s clean layouts and intuitive navigation ensure learners stay focused and engaged throughout the whole course.

Powered by Droip, Workademy gives you complete visual control over every design detail. You can customize every section, add advanced animations, set different fonts, layouts, etc, without touching code. From showcasing training programs to tracking progress and boosting learner engagement, Workademy is built to grow with your organization and help your teams succeed.

Key Features
Animations/Effects

Includes expressive animations. Tweak effects with a few clicks and capture your audience’s attention when they land on your website.

Color Styles

Color Styles are re-usable colors. They allow you to centrally manage how the colors on your site look. You can update the look and feel of your site in one easy step.

Components

Components offer reusability across pages, with changes applied to all instances. They also offer an easy way to create animations.

Carousel

Uses the built-in Carousel component to display multiple images in an interactive way. This carousel is easy to tweak to your own liking.

Responsive Design

Fully responsive out of the box. Visually tweak your breakpoints and design your site on a familiar free-form canvas.

Native Dynamic Content

Template content is connected to the Dynamic Content Manager, making it easy to customize and manage across your site with no setup needed.
