02Look · 2006

A minute of looking before a mark.

My first studio was a borrowed room in Budapest, shared with a sculptor. It had one chair, so I drew it, and drew what I thought a chair was.

It took me years to look first. Now every drawing starts the same way: one plumb line, one eye level, one circle around the whole shape.

  • 2008MA Fine Art, Painting · Hungarian University of Fine Arts
Overheard in a critique · 00:18 · Marta, Course 01Don’t draw yet. Look at it for a full minute.

03Gesture · 2010

Fast, before I could think.

A three-month residency brought me to Lisbon for the light, and I never went back. I drew in cafés, on the 28 tram, at the Ribeira market: thirty seconds a drawing, the whole chair in one breath.

Gesture comes first, so the drawing is alive before it’s correct.

Overheard in a critique · 02:10 · Priya, Course 01Your eye went to the handle. Stay with the whole shape.

04Measure · 2014

The pencil became a ruler.

My first solo show, “Mesa”, was 22 studies of one table. I measured everything at arm’s length with one eye closed: the seat is one pencil head, the back is two. Proportion is only honest counting.

  • 2014“Mesa” · first solo show, Galeria Graça, Lisbon
Overheard in a critique · 04:55 · Leila, Course 03Close one eye. Now hold the seat against the leg.

05Values · 2016

Three values, not seven.

After six years of evening classes, a student asked how I “actually see” a shadow. I answered with three values: the paper, one middle grey, one dark. I filmed the answer, and it became the first lesson of Course 01.

Overheard in a critique · 00:42 · Marta, Course 01Squint. Count the values. I count three.

06Finish · today

Leave the lost edges lost.

Now I film every lesson at one table under a north window in Graça, in the same even light from 9 to 4. And I watch every student’s final drawing myself: eight to twelve minutes, about that one drawing.

Today four painters film at Elena’s table under the north window in Graça, in the same even light from 9 to 4, so every course looks like the same room. Each one teaches a single medium.
