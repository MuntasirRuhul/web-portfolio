We collect as little about you as we can, keep it only as long as we need it and never sell it. This page says what we keep, why, and how to ask for it back.

01Who looks after your data

Elena Varga Atelier, Rua da Graça 4, 3º, 1170-165 Lisboa, Portugal, is responsible for the personal data described here. You can reach us about privacy through the contact page; choose “Something else” and we’ll answer within a month, usually much sooner.

02What we keep

  • Your account: your first name, email address, password (stored scrambled, never readable) and the level you chose when you joined.
  • Your courses: what you’ve bought, which lessons you’ve finished, and your receipts.
  • Your drawings: photos you upload for feedback, and Elena’s notes on them.
  • Your letters: what you write to us through the contact form, and our replies.
  • Basic site logs: the pages requested and the browser used, kept briefly to keep the site secure.

We don’t ask for your address, your birthday or anything about your health, and we don’t keep card numbers.

03Why we keep it

  • To run your account and give you the courses you bought (to perform our contract with you).
  • To send receipts and keep invoices (because the law requires it).
  • To send studio notes, only if you ticked the box (with your consent, which you can withdraw in one click from any email).
  • To keep the site secure and working (our legitimate interest).

04Who else sees it

We use a small number of trusted services to run the school: a payment provider, a video host for the lessons, an email service for receipts and studio notes, and a web host. Each only gets what it needs to do its job, under a written agreement, and stores data in the EU or under EU-approved safeguards.

We never sell your data or share it with advertisers.

05Cookies

The site uses one essential cookie to keep you logged in, and remembers on your device whether you chose “Keep me logged in”. We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies. If we ever add anonymous visit counting, we’ll ask first.

06How long we keep it

  • Your account and course history: while your account is open, then 12 months after you close it.
  • Invoices: 10 years, as Portuguese tax law requires.
  • Uploaded drawings: until you delete them or close your account.
  • Letters to the studio: two years after the last reply.
  • Site logs: 30 days.

07Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, download it, or have it deleted. You can object to how we use it, or ask us to stop for a while. You can close your account from your settings or by writing to us, and we’ll confirm once it’s done.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, you can complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), or to the authority where you live.

08Keeping it safe

The site is served over an encrypted connection, passwords are stored scrambled, and only Elena and the people who help her with student support can see your account. If something ever goes wrong, we’ll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

09Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we’ll update the date at the top, and if the change matters, we’ll email you before it takes effect.
