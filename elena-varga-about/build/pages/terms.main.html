These terms cover your account with Elena Varga Atelier, the courses you take and the work you share with us. We’ve tried to write them the way Elena teaches: plainly, one thing at a time.

01Who we are

Elena Varga Atelier is an online drawing school run by Elena Varga from her studio at Rua da Graça 4, 3º, 1170-165 Lisboa, Portugal. When these terms say “we” or “the studio”, they mean Elena and the small team that helps her. “You” means the person using the site.

By creating an account or buying a course you agree to these terms. If you don’t agree, please don’t use your account; you can still read the studio notes and watch lesson 1.

02Your account

  • You need to be 16 or older to open an account. Younger students are welcome with a parent or guardian who opens the account for them.
  • Give us an email address you check. We use it for receipts, course updates and replies from Elena.
  • Keep your password to yourself. An account is for one student; please don’t share it.
  • Tell us straight away at the contact page if you think someone else has used your account.

03Courses and access

When you buy a course you get access to its video lessons, exercises and printable sheets for as long as the course is offered, and for at least three years from the day you buy it. If we ever retire a course, we’ll tell you at least 60 days before and let you download the sheets.

Lesson 1 of every course is free and stays free. Live sessions and private critiques are booked separately, and their dates are confirmed by email.

04Prices and payment

Prices are shown in euros and include Portuguese VAT where it applies. You pay once per course; there are no hidden subscriptions. Payments are handled by our payment provider, and we never see or store your full card number.

Gift courses are paid for by the giver and can be redeemed by the person named on the gift, within 12 months.

05Changing your mind

If a course isn’t right for you, write to us within 14 days of buying it and we’ll refund you in full, whether or not you’ve started. This is in addition to your rights under EU consumer law, not instead of them.

Private critiques can be moved or cancelled up to 48 hours before they’re due; after that, the critique is used.

06Your drawings

Everything you draw is yours. When you upload a drawing for feedback, you let us store it and show it to Elena and, in group critiques, to the other students in that session. That’s all.

We will only show your work anywhere else, for example on this site or in a studio note, if you say yes in writing for that drawing. You can change your mind at any time and we’ll take it down.

07Elena’s lessons

The videos, drawings, sheets and texts on this site belong to Elena Varga. You may watch them, print the sheets and copy the exercises for your own practice, and you may teach what you learn. Please don’t resell, re-upload or share course files publicly.

08In the studio

Group sessions and comments work because people are kind about each other’s drawings. We may remove comments, or close an account, if someone is abusive, posts others’ work as their own, or uses the site to sell things. We’ll tell you why, and refund any course you haven’t started.

09Our responsibility

We work hard to keep the site running and the lessons accurate, but we can’t promise the site will never be down. Nothing in these terms limits the rights you have as a consumer, or our responsibility for harm we cause through negligence.

10Changes and law

If we change these terms, we’ll email you at least 30 days before the change takes effect, and you can close your account if you don’t agree. These terms are governed by Portuguese law, and you can bring a claim in the courts where you live.
