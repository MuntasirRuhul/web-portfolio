Twenty years of
learning to look.

Painter / Teacher

Budapest 1984 · Lisbon since 2010

01In her words

I don’t teach people to draw. I teach them [to look]: a full minute before a mark, three values instead of seven, and the nerve to leave [a lost edge lost.]

Elena V.Graça, Lisbon · January 2026
02Twenty years

A walk along the studio wall.

2006 → 2026
01 · Budapest
2006

A borrowed room in Budapest.

Shared with a sculptor. It had one chair, so she drew it, and drew what she thought a chair was.

Lesson · look first
02 · Budapest
2008

MA Fine Art, Painting.

Hungarian University of Fine Arts. One plumb line, one eye level, one circle around the whole shape.

Lesson · plumb lines
03 · Lisbon
2010

Lisbon, for the light.

A three-month residency she never left. Thirty-second drawings in cafés, on the 28 tram, at the Ribeira market.

Lesson · gesture
04 · Galeria Graça
2014

“Mesa”, her first solo show.

Twenty-two studies of one table at Galeria Graça. Everything measured at arm’s length, one eye closed.

Lesson · measure
05 · On camera
2016

One question, on camera.

A student asked how she “actually sees” a shadow. She filmed the answer: three values. It became Course 01.

Lesson · three values
06 · Graça, today
2026

The table under the north window.

Every lesson filmed in the same light, 9 to 4. Every final drawing watched by her.

Lesson · finish
03The January chair

Same chair. Every January.

Since 2006 she has drawn the chair from her first studio on a blank sheet, in the six stages every course teaches. Step through them.
